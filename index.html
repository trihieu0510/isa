Who We Are

More than an association —
a family.

The International Students Association (ISA) of The Chinese University of Hong Kong is a student-led organisation dedicated to supporting international students and celebrating the diverse cultures that make up our campus community.

From your first day in Hong Kong to graduation, ISA is here to help you settle in, make friends, and create a vibrant, inclusive student life — bridging international and local students alike.

  • A welcoming community for students from every corner of the world
  • Events, socials, and support throughout your CUHK journey
  • Run by the dedicated 17th Executive Committee
